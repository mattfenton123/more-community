Parkrun.

Free, weekly, timed 5k event every Saturday morning at Dunorlan Park. Walk, jog, run, volunteer or spectate.

Get Barcode Volunteer

Is this for you?

The most positive way to start your weekend. Hundreds of locals coming together purely for the joy of movement and community spirit.

  • Completely free forever
  • Walkers, joggers, and sprinters welcome
  • Family and buggy friendly

Typical Gathering

When: Every Saturday at 9:00 AM sharp

Where: Dunorlan Park Events Field

What: A timed 5k loop around the lake, fully supported by enthusiastic volunteers.
