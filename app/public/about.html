Our Story

From burnout
to belonging.

For years, I hid behind achievement, unhealthy patterns, and the noise of a life that looked functional on the outside but felt deeply disconnected underneath.

I pushed myself through endurance events and Ironman triathlons, constantly running from burnout. But everything changed when I stopped running alone and started running alongside others. Through shared challenges and honest connection, I found clarity and a completely different way of living.

"Life is meant to be experienced together."

more was built from that exact realization. It exists to help people step outside their comfort zones, find their people, and share unforgettable experiences in the real world.

Our Mission

A movement for the UK.

Every town in Britain has dozens of vibrant community groups — walking clubs, running crews, faith groups, entrepreneur meetups, volunteer teams, arts collectives. Yet, most are invisible online. Their members find them by word of mouth, or not at all.

We believe every shared experience should be easy to find and join. We're building a movement to make that happen — celebrating real-life connections, starting in Tunbridge Wells and scaling to every town in the country.

Free for leaders. Always. Because the champions who bring people together shouldn't have to pay for the privilege.

What We Believe

Our values.

Free Forever

Community leaders should never pay to serve their community. Our tools are free — no trials, no catches, no upgrades required.

Leader First

We exist to serve the people who bring people together. Every feature we build starts with the question: "Does this make leading easier?"

Real Life

We're not building another social network. We're building infrastructure for in-person connection. The screen is the starting point — not the destination.

Inclusive by Design

Every community type belongs here — walking, faith, sport, art, volunteering, entrepreneurship. If it brings people together, it has a home on more

Local Density

We go deep, not wide. One thriving town is worth more than a hundred empty cities. We build density before we build scale.

Protect Leaders

We enforce co-leadership, automate admin, and monitor wellbeing. Our platform doesn't just serve leaders — it protects them from burnout.
