How It Works

From brief to prototype.

1

Team Up

Form teams of 3-5. Don't know anyone? No problem — we match complementary skills on the day.

2

Build

48 hours. One problem brief. Your team builds a working prototype. Mentors are on hand throughout.

3

Pitch

Present your solution to a panel of judges, investors, and community leaders. Best projects win prizes and support to continue.

What's Different

Local problems.
Local impact.

Most hackathons are abstract exercises. Ours solve real problems — challenges set by TW Borough Council, local charities, NHS partners, and community organisations.

Past challenges have included loneliness isolation tools, community event discovery, and local business sustainability solutions. The best prototypes don't just win prizes — they get built for real.

"This isn't a weekend hobby. It's where the next local startup gets born."
