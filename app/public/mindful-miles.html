Mindful Miles.

Weekly walk and talk sessions in the beautiful Tunbridge Wells countryside. A safe space to connect, share, and enjoy the outdoors. No pace, no pressure — just presence.

Join Next Walk View Routes

Is this for you?

Mindful Miles was created for those who want to get out of the house, breathe some fresh air, and have genuine conversations without the pressure of a workout.

  • All fitness levels welcome
  • Friendly dogs encouraged
  • Focus on mental health & chat

Typical Gathering

When: Every Sunday morning at 10:00 AM

Where: Various trailheads (usually starting near Dunorlan Park)

What: A gentle 3-5km loop followed by coffee and pastries.
