Is this for you?

For the early risers and the distance seekers. If you own a good pair of waterproof boots and love a sweeping valley view, you belong here.

  • 10km+ distances at a brisk pace
  • Mud, hills, and unpredictable weather
  • Pub lunch always included

Typical Gathering

When: Alternating Saturdays at 8:00 AM

Where: High Weald AONB

What: A 15km challenging hike through the beautiful Kent countryside, ending at a historic country pub.
