The Problem

Leading is harder
than it should be.

The "Platform Tax"

Subscription platforms charge leaders £17 to £33 a month just for the privilege of keeping a group open, creating a barrier for grassroots clubs.

Invisible Discovery

If your group only lives in WhatsApp or Facebook Groups, new locals can't find you cleanly without fighting restrictive social feeds.

Fragmented Tools

Organisers are forced to stitch together separate apps: ticketing links for RSVPs, messy group threads for updates, and manual spreadsheets for rosters.

Leader Burnout

Clubs often end up dependent on one volunteer carrying admin, costs, and logistics alone. Sharing hosting cleanly prevents burnout.

The Solution

Tools that make
leading easier.

Fund Your Community

Run paid memberships, ticketed events, and local business sponsorships (80% straight to your club fund) via Faster Payments UK bank payouts - 5% platform fee (2% on Pro) plus Stripe fees.

Get Discovered

List your community in our town directory searchable by category, interest, and location so local members find you organically.

Events, QR & Carpools

Create one-off or recurring events with QR check-in, calendar feeds, digital passes, +1 companion spots, and built-in carpool seat sharing.

Built-in Chat

Dedicated community channels and direct messaging alongside your events - no burying logistics inside chaotic social threads.

Member CRM & CSV

Monitor Active, Engaged, Slipping, and Inactive members with verified check-in rosters and 1-click CSV exports of your community data.

Shared Co-Hosts

Invite trusted deputy co-hosts and collaborate with local specialists so no community has to be one person deep.

AI Studio & Flyers

Draft event pages with AI, export custom promotional flyers across 20 curated fonts, and welcome new joiners automatically.

Safety & Leader Pro

Optional Leader Pro (£16.50/mo or £165/yr) unlocks 2% ticket fees, 1-click safety & RAMS PDF declarations, digital member check-in waivers, and £5M organiser legal cover standards.
