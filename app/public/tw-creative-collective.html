Is this for you?

For anyone who makes things. Whether you paint, code, write, or design, this is a space to overcome creative blocks and collaborate.

  • Casual, messy, and creative
  • Opportunities to exhibit work
  • Skill-sharing workshops

Typical Gathering

When: Every other Thursday at 6:30 PM

Where: Local art cafes and studios

What: Drink & Draw sessions where we socialize, sketch, and critique each other's ongoing projects.
