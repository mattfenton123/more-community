YENTW Programme

From idea to launch.
In 12 weeks.

The Startup Accelerator is an intensive programme designed to help early-stage founders go from concept to a viable product. Mentorship, workshops, pitch coaching — and a Demo Day in front of local investors.

Apply for Next Cohort →

Programme Structure

12 weeks of building.

1-3

Validate

Problem discovery, customer research, market validation. Make sure you're solving a real problem for real people.

4-6

Build

MVP development, prototype creation, user testing. Get something real in front of actual users.

7-9

Grow

Go-to-market strategy, first customers, pricing, metrics that matter. Learn to grow with intention.

10-12

Pitch

Pitch preparation, investor intros, financial modelling. Culminating in Demo Day at The Pantiles.
