Is this for you?

A sanctuary in the city. We welcome complete beginners and seasoned yogis to breathe, stretch, and find their center together.

  • Vinyasa, Yin, and Restorative
  • Focus on breathwork and mindfulness
  • Bring your own mat

Typical Gathering

When: Wednesdays at 7:00 PM & Sundays at 8:00 AM

Where: The Common (Summer) / Local Hall (Winter)

What: A 60-minute guided flow focusing on mobility, strength, and deep relaxation.
