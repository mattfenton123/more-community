# More Community — 5-Minute Concierge Onboarding Cheatsheet

When a community organizer responds positively to an outreach message, execute this 5-minute white-glove onboarding process to ensure maximum delight and immediate adoption.

---

## The 5-Minute Checklist

### Minute 1: Pre-Fill Community Profile
1. Head to `morecommunity.app/create-community` (or create via Admin/Dashboard).
2. Enter their community name, category, and town (Tunbridge Wells).
3. Grab a high-res cover photo from their public Instagram or Facebook page.
4. Paste their existing bio/story from their social channels.

### Minute 2: Schedule Their Next 2 Meetups
1. Look up their next regular meeting date and time (e.g. *"Saturday 9:00 AM at Dunorlan Park"*).
2. Add the title, time, meeting point pin, and set capacity (e.g. 25).
3. Enable automated autopilot reminders (24h before & 2h before).

### Minute 3: Configure Guidelines & WhatsApp Fallback
1. Under **Settings → Guidelines**, select the appropriate 1-click preset pack:
   * *Active & Committed* (for sports/running)
   * *Respectful & Inclusive* (for social/wellbeing/parents)
   * *Outdoors & Safety* (for wild swimming/hiking)
2. If they have an existing WhatsApp group, paste their invite link into the **WhatsApp Integration** section as a fallback.

### Minute 4: Send Them Their Live Magic Link
Send a warm WhatsApp or DM with their personalized link:

> *"Hey [First Name]! Your community is all set up and ready to view here:*
> 
> 👉 `https://www.morecommunity.app/community/[id]`
> 
> *I’ve already added your photos and your upcoming meetup on [Day]. When you log in with your email, you’ll automatically see your **Leader Dashboard** with live attendee lists, QR check-in, and full control.*
> 
> *Take a look and let me know what you think!"*

### Minute 5: Transfer Leader Role
Once they sign up or log in:
1. Promote their user account to **Leader** on the dashboard.
2. Provide them the WhatsApp Growth Copy to paste into their existing group:
   > *"Hey everyone! We've set up a clean new event page for [Group Name] on More Community. You can RSVP in 1 tap for our next meetup here: https://www.morecommunity.app/community/[id]"*
