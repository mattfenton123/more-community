"The Economics of Community in Tunbridge Wells"

Topic: The £240 software penalty on volunteer hosts
Last week, I spoke with a community leader in Tunbridge Wells who organizes a free weekly walking group for 50+ local residents. She plans the routes. She scouts wheelchair and dog-friendly paths. She waits at the car park to make sure no nervous newcomer walks alone. Then she opened her laptop and showed me her annual bill: Meetup.com was charging her £228 a year. Think about that for a second. A volunteer, doing unpaid civic work that directly fights the UK's loneliness epidemic, being taxed over £200 a year just to list meeting dates. We built More Community (morecommunity.app) on three unbreakable principles: 1. 100% Free Forever for Leaders 2. Respect Member Attention (Zero spam feeds) 3.
