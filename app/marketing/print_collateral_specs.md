## 2. The Cafe Coaster & Table Talker

* **Format:** 94 mm Round or Square Beermat (1.4 mm Absorbent Pulpboard) OR A6 Acrylic Table Tent.
* **Target Coffee Shops:**
  * The Cake Shed (The Pantiles)
  * Even Flow (St John’s Rd)
  * Fine Grind (High St & The Pantiles)
  * Juliet’s Cafe (High St)
  * The Bicycle Bakery (Camden Rd)

### Side A: Member Discovery (Consumer Facing)
* **Headline:** Looking for your people in Tunbridge Wells?
* **Body:** Discover 30+ free local running clubs, walks, socials, and creative meetups in town.
* **Central Visual:** High-contrast dynamic QR Code linking to `https://www.morecommunity.app/discover`
* **Footer:** Scan to explore • *morecommunity.app*

### Side B: Leader Invitation (Organizer Facing)
* **Headline:** Run a local group or club?
* **Body:** Host your meetups for free with zero organizer fees, clean RSVPs, and QR check-in.
* **Call-to-Action:** Scan to claim your free group microsite → `https://www.morecommunity.app/create-community`
* **Footer:** Free Forever for Leaders • Made in Tunbridge Wells
