# More Community — Marketing & Growth Engine

Welcome to the **More Community Marketing & Leader Acquisition Hub**. This directory houses all strategic documents, target lead pipelines, outreach copy, objection playbooks, and print specifications for launching and scaling More Community across Royal Tunbridge Wells, West Kent, and beyond.

---

## Folder Structure

```
marketing/
├── README.md                          # This index and quick-start guide
├── pipeline_tracker.csv               # Top 25 local communities pipeline (ready for Excel/Sheets)
├── pipeline_tracker.md                # Human-readable pipeline with custom hooks & meeting details
├── leader_pitch_one_pager.md          # 1-page printable/shareable pitch document for community organizers
├── outreach_playbooks.md              # Multi-touch DM, Email, WhatsApp templates & objection handling
├── print_collateral_specs.md          # A5 Flyer, Cafe Coaster & Table Talker specs with print copy
└── leader_onboarding_cheatsheet.md    # 5-minute concierge onboarding guide when a leader says "Yes"
```

## Core Brand & Positioning Truths

1. **Brand Identity:** **More Community**
2. **Core Tagline:** *Find Your People, Connect in Real Life*
3. **Core Promise to Leaders:** **100% Free Forever.** No subscriptions, no ads, no organizer fees, no catch.
4. **North Star Metric:** **In-Person Check-Ins** (members meeting face-to-face in the real world).
