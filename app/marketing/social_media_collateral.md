## 4. Founder LinkedIn Thought Leadership Posts

### Post L1: "The £240 Tax on Community"
* **Author:** Matt Fenton, Founder
* **Topic:** The economics of community and why meetup software is fundamentally broken.

```text
Last week, I spoke with a community leader in Tunbridge Wells who organizes a free weekly walking group for 50+ local residents.

She plans the routes.
She scouts wheelchair and dog-friendly paths.
She waits at the car park to make sure no nervous newcomer walks alone.

Then she opened her laptop and showed me her annual bill:
Meetup.com was charging her £228 a year.

Think about that for a second.

A volunteer, doing unpaid civic work that directly fights the UK's loneliness epidemic, being taxed over £200 a year just to list meeting dates.

When she told me she was considering shutting the group down because she couldn't justify the cost, I knew we had to build something better.

We built More Community (morecommunity.app) on three unbreakable principles:

1. 100% Free Forever for Leaders: If you are organizing free meetups in your town, you will never pay us a single penny. No tiers, no surprise paywalls, no organizer fees.
2. Respect Member Attention: No spammy algorithm feeds. No doom-scrolling. Just clean RSVPs, accurate capacity limits, and automated calendar passes.
3. Focused on In-Person Check-Ins: Our North Star metric isn't daily screen time; it's how many humans in Kent actually met face-to-face in the real world.

If you know a community organizer in Tunbridge Wells or Kent who deserves better tools and zero fees, send them my way or tag them below.

Life is meant to be experienced together.

#Community #SocialImpact #TunbridgeWells #TechForGood #LonelinessEpidemic #FounderJourney #Kent
```

### Post L2: "Why Group Chats Fail Local Communities"
* **Author:** Matt Fenton, Founder
* **Topic:** Why WhatsApp is great for chat, but toxic for event coordination.

```text
WhatsApp is brilliant for texting your mates.
It is an absolute nightmare for running a 50-person club.

Here is the exact weekly cycle of every community host in the UK:

Monday: Host posts meetup details.
Tuesday: 42 people reply "Can't make it this week!", burying the location pin.
Wednesday: 5 people reply "What time are we meeting again?"
Friday: Host spends 45 minutes manually counting thumbs-up emojis to figure out if the pub table has enough seats.
Saturday: 3 people go to the wrong car park.

We didn't build More Community to kill WhatsApp chats. We built it to give organizers their sanity back:

• Drop 1 clean link into your WhatsApp chat.
• Members click RSVP in 1 second.
• Automatic waitlists trigger when the venue cap is reached.
• Autopilot push notifications send the meeting pin 2 hours before.
• Host scans attendee QR passes on their phone in 1 second.

Technology should get out of the way so people can just enjoy each other's company.

Have you experienced the "WhatsApp headcount chaos"? How does your local club handle it?

#CommunityManagement #ProductDesign #LocalCommunities #Productivity #StartupLife #TunbridgeWells
```
