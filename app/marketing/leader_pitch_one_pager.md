## 5 Superpowers Built for Community Organizers

### 1. 🌐 A Dedicated Public Microsite (In 60 Seconds)
* Your custom link: `morecommunity.app/community/your-group`
* Showcase your story, photos, schedule, meeting spots, and guidelines with zero coding.
* Fast, mobile-optimized experience that works on every smartphone.

### 2. 🎟️ Frictionless RSVPs & 1-Second QR Check-In
* Set attendee limits, ticket prices, and automatic waitlists in 3 clicks.
* Automated push reminders send before every meetup so members actually show up.
* Scan member QR codes in 1 second on your phone to track real-world attendance.

### 3. 📢 Broadcast Announcements (Without the Chat Chaos)
* Post announcements that go straight to your members' notification feeds.
* Meeting locations, weather updates, and key dates never get buried under 150 chat messages.
* Built-in WhatsApp bridge to keep your group chat in sync without the clutter.

### 4. 🛡️ Community Norms & Co-Leadership Safeguards
* Set clear group guidelines (*Respectful & Inclusive, Safety, Cadence*) in 1 click.
* Promote trusted members to **Co-Leaders** to share the hosting load and prevent burnout.
* Privacy controls: Public, Private, or Require Approval for newcomers.

### 5. 💷 Zero Platform Fees & Optional Monetisation
* Keep your meetups 100% free, or collect ticket/membership revenue with zero platform cut.
* Curate high-margin weekend retreats and trips from our partner marketplace if you wish.
