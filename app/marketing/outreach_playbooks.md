# More Community — Multi-Touch Outreach Playbooks

This guide contains word-for-word outreach sequences and objection handling scripts for converting target community leaders in Tunbridge Wells and West Kent.

---

## 4-Touch Conversion Sequence

```
Touch 1 (Day 1)   ──>   Touch 2 (Day 3)   ──>   Touch 3 (Day 6)   ──>   Touch 4 (Day 10)
Warm Compliment         Showcase Link           In-Person Coffee        Graceful Soft Close
```

---

### Touch 1: The Initial Warm Compliment (Instagram DM / LinkedIn / Email)
*Objective: Build rapport, compliment their community, and ask permission to send a draft link.*

> **Subject:** Quick question about [Group Name] in Tunbridge Wells 🙌
>
> *"Hey [First Name], love what you’ve built with [Group Name] in Tunbridge Wells — it's such an important community for the town!*
>
> *I’m Matt, local to TW. We recently launched **More Community** (morecommunity.app) — a platform built specifically to support local leaders like you.*
>
> *We give you your own dedicated microsite, automatic RSVP lists with capacity limits (no more hand-counting in WhatsApp chats), automated reminders, and QR check-in — completely free forever, with zero organizer fees or catches.*
>
> *I’ve actually put together a quick draft microsite for [Group Name] so you can see what it looks like. Mind if I send you the link? No pressure at all, just keen to support what you’re doing!"*

---

### Touch 2: The Value Demonstration (48 Hours Later)
*Objective: Send their custom pre-built link and show how simple it is.*

> *"Hey [First Name]! Here is the preview link I put together for [Group Name]:*
> 
> 👉 `https://www.morecommunity.app/community/[id]`
> 
> *It already has your photos, upcoming meetup details, and RSVP buttons set up. You can claim it in 1 click, edit anything you like, and use it to manage your next meetup with zero stress.*
> 
> *Takes about 60 seconds to claim, or I'm happy to walk you through it over a 5-minute coffee at The Pantiles if you're around this week!"*

---

### Touch 3: The Coffee / Phone Invite (4 Days Later)
*Objective: Transition from digital to in-person connection.*

> *"Hey [First Name], hope you had a great week!*
> 
> *I’m grabbing coffee in town on Thursday morning (around Even Flow / The Cake Shed). If you have 15 minutes, I'd love to buy you a flat white and hear more about your vision for [Group Name] this year.*
> 
> *Let me know if Thursday or Friday works for you!"*

---

### Touch 4: The Graceful Soft Close (7 Days Later)
*Objective: Leave the door wide open with zero sales pressure.*

> *"Hey [First Name]! No worries at all if you're flat out right now — I know how busy running a community gets.*
> 
> *The [Group Name] microsite is saved for you whenever you’re ready: https://www.morecommunity.app/community/[id]*
> 
> *If you ever get tired of chasing RSVPs in WhatsApp or paying third-party fees, we’re here to help you anytime. Rooting for your next meetup!"*

---

## Word-for-Word Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "We already have a WhatsApp group."
* **Underlying feeling:** "I don't want to force my members to move or disrupt what works."
* **Script:**
  > *"We love WhatsApp! More Community isn't meant to replace your chat — it complements it. You drop your More Community link into WhatsApp, and members click RSVP in 1 second. You get exact attendee headcounts, and people don't have to scroll through 100 messages to find the meeting time and postcode."*

### Objection 2: "Is it really free forever? What's the catch?"
* **Underlying feeling:** "Is this a free trial that turns into a £20/month subscription?"
* **Script:**
  > *"100% free forever. We believe community leaders are doing public good and should never pay to serve their town. We will never charge leaders a monthly fee or take cuts from your free events. Our business model is supported by optional partner retreats and local brand sponsorships."*

### Objection 3: "Will my members have to download another app?"
* **Underlying feeling:** "People hate downloading new apps from the App Store."
* **Script:**
  > *"Not at all! Your community microsite works instantly in any web browser on iOS and Android. Members can view event details and RSVP without having to install anything from the App Store. If they want the native app experience, they can install it in 1 tap, but it's never required."*

### Objection 4: "I don't have time to set up another platform."
* **Underlying feeling:** "I have a full-time job and family; admin exhausts me."
* **Script:**
  > *"I completely understand — community admin is exhausting. That's why I've already drafted your profile and first event for you. All you have to do is log in, hit 'Claim', and you're live. I can also do it for you on a 5-minute screen share or over a quick coffee in town."*
