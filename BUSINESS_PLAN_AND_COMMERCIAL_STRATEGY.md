# More Community: Commercial Strategy & Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary & Revenue Architecture
More Community provides independent clubs, outdoor groups, and local social circles with an all-in-one platform for ticketing, RSVPs, chats, and member management.

Rather than charging prohibitive software subscriptions to non-profit organizers or charging extractive ticket surcharges, More aligns its commercial model with community vitality through **Local Partnerships, Collaborative Guest Sessions, and Club Sponsorships**.

---

## 2. Core Commercial Products & Split Structure

### Phase 1: Local Club Partner & Member Perk (£15 / month)
- **Target**: Neighborhood cafes, independent running/sports shops, dog groomers, local physios, yoga studios, and bakeries.
- **Offering**:
  - Featured partner badge on the community profile page.
  - Exclusive member perk listing (e.g., "15% off post-run coffee", "Free trial class").
  - Inclusion in new member welcome packs and event details.
- **Commercial Split**:
  - **80% (£12 / month)** -> Directly to the Community Leader's Club Fund (equipment, room hire, subsidies).
  - **20% (£3 / month)** -> More Community platform fee.
- **Why Local Businesses Pay**:
  - Highly qualified, hyper-local audience (e.g., 200 active runners in the exact same postcode).
  - Outperforms generic Meta/Google local ads with zero ad-blindness.
  - Real footfall and direct customer loyalty driven by genuine community endorsement.

### Phase 2: Headline Sponsor Tier (£49 / month) & Town Bundles
*Trigger: To be rolled out automatically as soon as initial local businesses sign up for £15/mo tiers.*

#### A. £49 / month Headline Sponsor Tier
- **Target**: Well-funded local businesses (estate agents, local solicitors, regional craft breweries, established gyms, equipment retailers).
- **Offering**:
  - Premier "Headline Partner" banner at the very top of the community page.
  - Dedicated sponsor callout on ticket confirmation emails and push notifications.
  - Pinned announcement post per quarter.
- **Commercial Split**:
  - **£37 / month (75%)** -> Leader Club Fund.
  - **£12 / month (25%)** -> More Community platform fee (4x net revenue increase per converted business).

#### B. Multi-Club Town Bundles
- **Target**: Businesses wishing to reach an entire town or interest vertical (e.g., all 4 walking groups in Hebden Bridge, or all outdoor clubs in Sheffield).
- **Offering**:
  - Category Exclusivity Bundle (e.g., £99 - £149 / month across 3 to 5 complementary communities).
  - Cross-community perk recognition (a single discount code or card recognized across all network clubs).
- **Commercial Split**:
  - 70% pooled and distributed to participating club leaders based on active member count.
  - 30% retained by More Community as the network aggregator.

## 3. Collaborative Guest Sessions & Experiences (Option A Hybrid)

### Self-Declared Collaborative Host Model
- Replaces rigid liability gatekeeping with a transparent, mutual-trust pitch system.
- Instructors, mountain leaders, nutritionists, and guest speakers can propose sessions directly to any community leader with:
  - Session description and proposed ticket price or free structure.
  - Qualifications, experience, or certifications (self-declared).
  - Proof of public liability insurance (optional or self-certified).
  - Proposed revenue share (e.g., 70% instructor / 30% club fund).
- **Leader Experience & Anti-Hassle Guarantee**:
  - No cold DMs, spam, or unsolicited phone calls.
  - All pitches arrive quietly in the Leader Dashboard inbox with full session details.
  - Single-tap actions: "Accept & Add to Calendar", "Negotiate", or "Decline Quietly" (no awkward social friction).
  - Global toggle in Community Settings: "Accept Guest Sessions & Sponsor Pitches" (can be turned off at any time).

## 4. Leader Incentive Flywheel: The Unpaid Local Sales Force

1. **Zero Churn Lock-In**:
   - When a leader earns £12 to £50+ per month directly into their More Community Club Fund from local sponsors, they will never migrate their group back to WhatsApp, Facebook Groups, or Meetup (which pay them £0 and charge high fees).
2. **Organic Local Sales Team**:
   - Leaders become More's greatest sales champions. When grabbing a post-walk coffee or buying group gear, leaders naturally say: *"If you want to sponsor our Saturday group and offer our 150 members a perk, join us on More for £15/mo."*
3. **Low-Friction Member Onboarding**:
   - Service providers and sponsors do not register through clunky separate B2B portals. They join as regular community members, keeping friction zero and preserving authentic human connection.

## 7. The Legal Reality of Paid Communities & The Leader Pro PLI Moat

### The "Volunteer" vs. Charging Reality
A critical point of legal exposure for community organizers in the UK and worldwide:
- **Free Informal Gatherings**: If an organizer runs a 100% free casual social walk where friends chat, courts generally view it as an informal gathering, and volunteer protections (such as the UK SARAH Act 2015) offer standard defence.
- **The Moment Money is Charged**: The second an organizer charges an entry fee, ticket price, or recurring membership (even if framed as a "donation" or "subsidy"), the legal relationship shifts to a **contractual and commercial service**. The organizer assumes a strict legal **Duty of Care**.

### Why Waivers and Disclaimers Do Not Shield Negligence
- Under the **UK Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (s.2(1))** and the **Consumer Rights Act 2015 (s.65)**, it is legally impossible for an organizer to exclude or restrict liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence.
- If a participant twists an ankle, suffers hypothermia on an un-checked route, or damages third-party property (e.g. open livestock gate), the organizer can be sued personally. Defence costs alone can bankrupt an individual volunteer.

### Landowner and Venue Mandates
- Local councils, nature reserves, National Parks, and village halls mandate proof of **?5M to ?10M Public Liability Insurance (PLI)** before granting permissions or permits.
- Standalone broker policies cost individual clubs **?100 to ?150 / year**, requiring tedious underwriting forms, annual declarations, and policy exclusions.

### Leader Pro as the Platform Retention Moat
By bundling **Built-in Public Liability Insurance (PLI)** directly into Leader Pro (?16.50 / month or ?165 / year):
- More absorbs the terrifying legal and paperwork headache for community leaders.
- Leaders receive instant ?5M / ?10M policy certificates for council venues, automated RAMS packs, attendee digital waivers, and 2% platform ticket fees (saving 60% on ticketing).
- This creates an invincible competitive moat: organizers cannot migrate to WhatsApp, Facebook, or Meetup because none of those platforms provide group insurance or council compliance.

## 8. Two-Way Scam & Trust Protection Architecture

To ensure the platform cannot be exploited by either rogue guest partners OR rogue community leaders:

1. **Universal Post-Event Ticket Escrow**:
   - Applies symmetrically to both Community Leaders and Guest Session Partners.
   - All ticket payments are collected via Stripe and held in platform escrow until **24 hours after the scheduled event completes**.
   - If a host no-shows, cancels, or misrepresents the session, attendees receive an automatic single-tap 100% refund. Scammers have zero ability to cash out.

2. **Sponsor Monthly Activity Lock**:
   - Sponsor contributions (?15/mo or ?49/mo) are disbursed on a rolling 30-day payout cycle and require an **Active Community Status**.
   - If a community leader goes dormant (no events hosted or scheduled for 30 days) or deletes the group, the sponsor subscription is automatically paused/refunded and the sponsor is alerted. A bad actor cannot collect sponsor money without running an active community.

3. **Stripe Identity Verification & KYC for All Withdrawals**:
   - Before any leader or instructor can withdraw even ?1 from their Club Fund or ticket payout, they must complete verified Stripe Connect onboarding (Government Photo ID, National Insurance / Tax ID, Proof of Address, and bank account).
   - Anonymous fraudulent accounts cannot withdraw funds.

4. **Admin Multi-Persona Simulator**:
   - Platform super-admins can toggle their live perspective across all 5 operational roles (Admin Console, Leader View, Member View, Local Sponsor View, Experience Host View).
   - Enables instant auditing of how any community, ticket pass, or sponsor perk appears to end-users in real time.
