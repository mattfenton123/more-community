Join Dialogue Events

Is this for you?

We bring together the rich tapestry of cultures and beliefs in Tunbridge Wells through the universal language of food and dialogue.

  • All faiths and non-faiths welcome
  • Focus on shared humanity
  • Amazing potluck dinners

Typical Gathering

When: First Thursday of every month at 7:00 PM

Where: Various community halls (rotates)

What: A shared multicultural feast followed by an open, respectful dialogue on a specific life topic.
