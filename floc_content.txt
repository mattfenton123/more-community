Human beings are at our best when we come together.
At our core, we are wired for connection – to share, to 
explore, to belong. FLOC. is built on that timeless truth. 
It’s a community-driven travel app designed to bring 
people, plans, and purpose together in one place.
With FLOC., we’re not just launching an app – we’re 
helping people evolve to their ancestral best: humans 
centred in and by community.
Whether it’s a wellness retreat, a hiking trip, a golf break 
or a fashionista city escape, FLOC. empowers individuals 
to ﬁnd their people and share real experiences together.
Our platform combines the inspiration of Instagram with 
the connection of WhatsApp – but reimagined through a 
lens of purpose and travel.

Patreon
Community Building
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
Subscription-based platform that allows 
creators to monetise content by oﬀering 
tiered membership options to fans, who gain 
access to exclusive content and experiences. 
Revenue generated through a percentage 
fee from each transaction.
●
Empowers creators to build loyal, monetised communities.
●
Deep sense of connection between creator and audience.
●
Oﬀers ﬂexibility in how creators engage (posts, video, audio, messaging).
●
Provides a sustainable income stream based on recurring revenue.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
Discovery is limited – users must already know who they’re looking for.
●
Not optimised for real-time experiences or in-person events.
●
Community interaction is often one-way (creator to audience).
●
Not mobile-native in terms of UI/UX ﬂuidity.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
Build tools that give Community Leaders control and revenue potential.
●
Prioritise two-way interaction, not just broadcasting.
●
Include strong discovery mechanisms so communities can grow 
organically.

Discord
Community Building
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
Freemium model with optional paid features 
(Discord Nitro). Initially built for gamers but 
now widely used across interest-based 
communities, with private servers and tiered 
access.
●
Highly active, self-organising communities.
●
Real-time chat (text, voice, video).
●
Channels enable structured conversations.
●
Strong feeling of “ownership” among community members.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
UX can be overwhelming and cluttered for casual users.
●
Not purpose-built for travel or real-world coordination.
●
Discovery remains a challenge unless invited directly.
●
Hard for brands to communicate at scale without seeming intrusive.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
Make real-time communication central but simple.
●
Structure community interaction through channels (e.g. trips, dates, 
themes).
●
Build in onboarding tools that feel intuitive to non-tech-savvy users.
●
Allow space for authentic brand engagement without disruption.

Substack
Community Building
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
Substack is a subscription-based 
newsletter platform that enables writers 
to publish directly to their audience and 
monetise through paid subscriptions. 
Substack takes a 10% cut of all 
subscription revenue. It positions itself as 
an independent publishing tool, 
empowering creators to build personal 
media businesses without ads or 
algorithmic interference.
●
Creator-ﬁrst model gives full control to individual writers.
●
Built-in monetisation enables earning from day one.
●
High engagement from readers subscribing to niche content.
●
Comment threads oﬀer lightweight interaction and feedback.
●
Discovery tools and cross-promotion drive audience growth.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
Limited community interactivity beyond basic comments.
●
No shared spaces for interest-based group connection.
●
No features for travel, events, or real-world interaction.
●
Platform identity takes a backseat to individual brands.
●
Primarily a one-way content broadcast, not conversation.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
Monetisation tools should support niche expertise.
●
Blend individual creators with shared community spaces.
●
Keep onboarding and creation simple and intuitive.
●
Build strong discovery tools to spark new connections.

Vamoos
Travel Management
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
B2B SaaS platform for tour operators 
and travel companies. Oﬀers a 
white-label mobile app for itinerary 
sharing, document management, maps, 
and messaging.
●
Clear focus on enhancing the customer journey through tech.
●
Strong trip coordination tools and itinerary visualisation.
●
Simpliﬁes admin-heavy tasks for tour companies.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
No community-building functionality.
●
Not designed for peer-to-peer sharing or engagement.
●
Lacks personalisation, content inspiration, or social sharing.
●
Limited brand identity outside of white-labelled clients.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
Robust travel tools are a must: itineraries, documents, updates.
●
Layer this with social, emotional, and community-driven functionality.
●
FLOC. should feel like a shared experience, not just logistics.

Lato Travel
Travel Management
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
Tailored travel design platform enabling 
users to create personalised trips 
through curated templates and vendor 
partnerships. Primarily B2B with 
customisation services.
●
Clean UX with a high-end travel feel.
●
Useful for curating themed or niche experiences.
●
Appeals to travel advisors and premium clientele.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
No social features or direct community functionality.
●
Not mobile-app-ﬁrst: limited engagement outside of desktop/web.
●
Discovery and group collaboration are not core features.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
There’s appetite for curated experiences.
●
FLOC. should allow Community Leaders to design themed trips easily.
●
Elevate the UX to feel premium and intuitive, even for non-professionals.
●
Build native mobile-ﬁrst journeys that ﬁt group decision-making.

WeTravel
Travel Operating System
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
B2B payment and booking platform for 
group travel organisers. Oﬀers 
registration forms, payment collection, 
CRM features, and ﬁnancial tools.
●
Eﬃcient ﬁnancial management for group travel.
●
Streamlined admin tools, organisers can launch and manage trips easily.
●
Well-integrated backend functionality.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
Lacks front-end user engagement or inspiration tools.
●
Not designed for community interaction, content, or social planning.
●
No mobile app – desktop-focused.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
Embed strong organisational and payment tools into the FLOC. 
backend.
●
But centre the user experience on connection and community, not 
just admin.
●
A mobile-ﬁrst approach will gives an edge.

TravelTek
Travel Operating System
BUSINESS MODEL
STRENGTHS
Enterprise-level travel operating system. 
Provides a wide array of tools for travel 
agencies and tour operators including 
booking engines, CRM, and inventory 
management.
●
Comprehensive backend infrastructure.
●
Supports large-scale operations and integrations.
●
Scalable and versatile for travel businesses.
WEAKNESSES / GAPS
●
Zero community or user-facing functionality.
●
Complex, heavy systems not ﬁt for community-led or individual use.
●
Not brand-expressive or designed for shared experience.
LEARNINGS FOR FLOC.
●
There’s a future potential to acquire/partner with an operating 
system like TravelTek.
●
FLOC. must be user-led, content-rich, and emotionally engaging; the 
opposite of legacy systems.
●
Any acquisition must enhance functionality without compromising 
the UX.
