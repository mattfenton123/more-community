A-Z Challenge.

Complete running routes from A to Z across the borough. All abilities welcome, but we love a good challenge and a post-run pint.

Start Running Leaderboard

Is this for you?

We are gamifying fitness. It doesn't matter how fast you run, what matters is checking off every letter of the alphabet across Tunbridge Wells.

  • Pacing groups for all speeds
  • Gamified milestone tracking
  • High-energy social atmosphere

Typical Gathering

When: Every Tuesday evening at 6:30 PM

Where: Starting at The Pantiles

What: A 5k to 10k route (depending on the letter of the week) followed by a pub trip for a well-earned drink.
