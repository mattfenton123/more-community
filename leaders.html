The Problem

Leading is harder
than it should be.

💸

The "Meetup Tax"

Platforms charge leaders £17-33 a month just for the privilege of organizing a group. This creates a massive financial barrier for grassroots communities.

👻

Invisible Discovery

If you build a community on WhatsApp or Facebook Groups, you're practically invisible. You rely entirely on word-of-mouth or fighting restrictive algorithms.

🧩

Fragmented Tools

Leaders are forced to cobble together multiple apps—Eventbrite for RSVPs, WhatsApp for messy chats, and spreadsheets for tracking. There's no single "home."

🔥

Severe Burnout

Communities frequently become "one person deep." The sheer amount of manual admin combined with solo-leadership leads to massive burnout for organizers.

The Solution

Tools that make
leading easier.

📍

Get Discovered

Your community listed in the directory, searchable by interest, location, and activity. New members find you — you don't have to find them.

📅

Event Management

Create one-off or recurring events with automatic reminders. Track RSVPs. No more chasing people on WhatsApp.

💬

Built-in Chat

Group messaging that lives alongside your community — not buried in a separate app. Share updates, coordinate, connect.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

See attendance trends, member growth, and engagement data. Know what's working and where you need support.

👥

Co-Leadership

Add deputy leaders. Share the load. No community should be one person deep. Our platform enforces resilience.

🤖

Smart Automation

Auto-create recurring events. Auto-send reminders. Auto-welcome new members. You focus on the community — we handle the admin.
