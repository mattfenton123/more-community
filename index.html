Our Mission

Life is better when
we share it.

Every town in the UK is full of incredible people, vibrant energy, and shared passions. From early-morning run clubs to weekend arts collectives, the magic happens when we step away from our screens and come together in person.

We are a movement dedicated to celebrating and amplifying these real-life connections. We empower local leaders by removing all barriers to organizing, making their groups findable, welcoming, and thriving. Free for community champions. Always.

"We aren't building a platform. We're championing the joy of gathering together."

We provide the foundation for discovery, gatherings, and shared purpose—proving that real-life, face-to-face connection makes us happier, healthier, and more inspired.

The Founder From burnout to belonging

My Story

Everything changed when I stopped running from myself.

For years, I was trying to fit in rather than understand who I really was. I hid behind achievement, alcohol, unhealthy patterns, and the noise of a life that looked functional on the outside but felt disconnected underneath.

Through challenge, community, and honest self-reflection, I found clarity, purpose, and a completely different way of living.

more community was built from that journey — to help others step outside their comfort zones and realise they are capable of far more than they think.
